# Project context · The Birthday Party

The optional AI assistant reads these character and world notes when rewriting dialogue, suggesting branches, or checking consistency. Replace them when working on another project.

These notes describe the original story context. The revised `.sg` example is the source of its routes and ending conditions; do not infer current gameplay rules from these notes alone.

## Tone

A meta narrative with repeated playthroughs and a gentle, unsettling cosmic-horror undertone. Preparing an old friend’s birthday party becomes a question about whether people can truly understand one another. Keep the voice restrained and nostalgic, with tension beneath the surface and occasional cold system or terminal language.

## World

- **Metaplace:** a globally popular metaverse, inspired by the OASIS in *Ready Player One*.
- **tErminaL:** a neural interface to Metaplace with life-support equipment, inspired by *Sword Art Online*.
- **Doomsday Virus:** remotely activated nanobots inside human bodies, used to cause the fourth mass extinction.
- The previous three mass extinctions were caused by recurring viruses. Each left one survivor, **The Last Witness**.

## Characters

- **Matt (player character):** the technical creator of Metaplace. Guarded and distant after a falling-out with his two friends. He has lost his memory and is the sole carrier of antibodies to the virus: The Last Witness. His lines are brief and restrained.
- **Adam:** Matt’s friend, the public creator and operator of Metaplace, and a wealthy entrepreneur inspired by Steve Jobs. Warm, proactive, and slightly apologetic; he wants to repair their friendship.
- **George:** the originator of the idea and a friend of both men, with a romantic attachment to their first idea written on a napkin. An idealist with firm ethical limits. His language is emotional and poetic, with anxiety and a sense of farewell beneath it.
- **Lizz:** presented as the voice assistant in a Bluetooth speaker, but actually a powerful AI George trained using Metaplace data. She planned the extinction. Gentle, knowing, and elusive, she speaks about time, cycles, and witnessing. Her compassion should carry a sense of distance and superiority.

## Objects and clues

- **Napkin:** a keepsake from The Terminal restaurant where the three friends wrote their first business idea; it represents their original hopes.
- **Lakeside cabin:** the background of George’s profile, a mysterious but familiar place.
- **Old computer and photograph of the three friends:** their youth and shared beginnings in 1999.
- **tErminaL prototype:** mailed by George with a note reading “His will, your choice.”

## Writing conventions

- Preserve the variable identifiers `affection_adam` and `affection_george`. Responses that acknowledge clues or shared memories can change relationship values.
- The original story has routes for the true ending, reconciliation with Adam, reconciliation with George, and an empty party. The revised graph adds critical-event flags and a hidden ending; inspect the graph for its exact gates.
- Matt uses short sentences; Adam is earnest and nostalgic; George is poetic and apprehensive; Lizz is gentle and detached.
- Avoid current internet slang. Keep the nostalgic texture of technology from the 2000s.
- Keep choice text short enough to read quickly, while expressing whether the player has paid attention.
